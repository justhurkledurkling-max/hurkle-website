# Etsy shop launch checklist

Etsy requires a real name, government ID (for payment verification), and bank account details for payouts —
account creation and identity/payment verification have to be done by you directly on etsy.com. I can't do this
part for you. Everything else (copy, policies, listing structure) I can draft.

## 1. Create the shop (you do this — 15 min)
1. Go to etsy.com/sell → **Open your Etsy shop**.
2. Shop language / country / currency: English / your country / USD (or your local currency).
3. **Shop name**: `HurkleDurkling` (Etsy strips spaces/punctuation — confirm it's available before settling on it;
   have 2–3 backups ready, e.g. `HurkleDurklingShop`, `HurkleDurklingStudio`).
4. Skip "stock your shop" for now — no listings yet.
5. Set up billing: choose how you pay Etsy fees (card).
6. Set up payments: connect a bank account for payouts, verify your identity (Etsy legally requires this
   for anyone receiving payments — have a driver's license/passport and your bank routing + account number ready).
7. Confirm your shop is live but set to "Open" only once the first listing is ready — you can finish steps 2–4
   below before flipping that switch.

## 2. Shop settings (you paste, I can draft the text)
- **Shop icon**: small square logo — can reuse the site's mark once art is ready.
- **Shop banner**: optional, 1200x300ish.
- **About section**: story + photo. Draft: see `docs/brand-guide.md` (once written) or reuse the "Our story"
  copy already on justhurkledurkling.com.
- **Shop policies**: Etsy uses shop-wide policies now (not per-listing). Key ones for a digital-download shop:
  - **Returns & exchanges**: digital items are typically marked non-returnable (standard for the category).
  - **Privacy policy**: Etsy provides a template; link to it or write a short one — happy to draft.
  - See `docs/licensing-terms.md` for the buyer-facing usage license (what they can/can't do with the file) —
    this goes in each listing description, not the Etsy policy section.

## 3. First listing (once a product exists)
- Use `docs/etsy-listing-template.md` per product in `products/<slug>/listing.md`.
- Upload the PDF from `products/<slug>/pdf/` as the digital file.
- Category: Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Coloring Books, or similar (Etsy will suggest as you type).
- Price: research 3–5 comparable "printable coloring page set" listings for a starting anchor.

## 4. After the first listing goes live
- Add the real Etsy link to `index.html` (replace the `#` placeholder in the "Find us" section) and push.
- Set up Etsy's free "shop announcement" + a pinned listing once you have 3+ products.
