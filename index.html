our story

A studio that started with one messy sketchbook

Hurkle Durkling began as doodles in the margins — mushroom houses, wandering foxes, gardens that don't exist anywhere but here. Somewhere along the way, those doodles turned into pages worth sharing.

Every set is drawn by hand first, then scanned and tidied up so the lines stay crisp no matter how many times you print them. No two pages come out quite the same, and that's the whole point.
